The QA harness verifies the tag renders for all 10 codes in both Arabic and French, with no false positive on a normal code.
- The governorate control is a bonus selector, not a result filter: selecting `Sousse` adds +7 % only to rows whose `gov === "Sousse"` and `prep === false`; it does not hide other rows (verified: 246 rows still shown). Search by code, licence, institution and governorate (French or Arabic) filters correctly.
